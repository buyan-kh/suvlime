## Name

## Subtitle

Track peptides, GLP-1s, and supplements

## Description

Take your stuff. Get gains.

SuppsAI is the dumb-simple tracker for peptides, GLP-1s, and supplements. Build your stack, log every dose, calculate vial math, rotate injection spots, and ask an AI coach plain-English questions about your routine.

What you get:

- A Today screen that shows what to take and what is already done.
- A stack tracker for peptides, GLP-1s, vitamins, and supplements.
- Reconstitution math that turns vial strength into U-100 syringe ticks.
- A plain-English compound library with evidence ratings.
- A coach that knows your stack and helps you think through timing, habits, and reminders.
- Progress charts, streaks, check-ins, and simple weekly recaps.

SuppsAI is for tracking, education, and organization. It does not diagnose, treat, or prescribe. Talk to a licensed clinician before starting or changing any protocol.
